    About CloudLearn

    Free learning for everyone, powered by the cloud.

    Our Mission

    CloudLearn makes quality learning free and available to every student. Because it runs on the cloud, there is nothing to install: open the website on any phone or laptop, watch lessons, and your progress is saved automatically.

    Why Cloud-Based Learning?

    The benefits of running education on the cloud.

    🌍

    Accessibility

    Students can reach content from any device with an internet connection.

    📈

    Scalability

    Cloud hosting scales automatically as the number of students grows.

    💰

    Free Cost

    All courses are free, and the site runs on free cloud hosting with no servers to buy or maintain.

    🔒

    Reliability

    Cloud providers offer backups, high availability and security.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is CloudLearn really free?

    Yes. Every course is free, and creating an account is free.

    Do I need to install anything?

    No. CloudLearn runs in your web browser on any phone, tablet or computer.

    How are lessons completed?

    Each course video is split into lessons. A lesson is completed automatically once you have watched its part of the video. Skipping ahead doesn't count.

    Can I continue on another device?

    Yes. Your progress is saved in the cloud, so just login on any device and continue where you left off.
